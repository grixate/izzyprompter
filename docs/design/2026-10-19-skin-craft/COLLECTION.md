# Skin craft — collection candidate

19 October 2026. Local working candidate, prepared for owner review. Audience fit and physical-device acceptance are not established.

The shared catalog has Original plus 25 optional PRO candidates: the preserved fifteen, six primary additions and four reserve directions implemented for comparison. Implementing the reserve directions is not an automatic decision to ship all 25. The owner should select the final set after reviewing the actual objects; the participant protocol records the remaining audience work.

- **Clear:** transparent pale shell, internal frame and exposed belt/pulleys. Chalk, grey-green, vermilion.
- **Spool:** violet transparent shell, large main reel and smaller take-up, tape path, guide and pinch. Smoke violet and acid.
- **Glaze:** rounded blush ceramic, raw warm foot, sculpted oxblood key.
- **Pop:** butter shell, three broad coloured sectors and a squared key; a graphic alternative to simulated hardware.
- **Moss:** mineral green body, walnut rim and pale quiet centre.
- **Mono:** matte ink, broken chalk ring, no simulated metal.
- **Loom (reserve):** stitched oxblood fabric around a teal enamel squircle.
- **Arcade (reserve):** aqua case, four rubber pads and a cherry key.
- **Observatory (reserve):** midnight enamel, brass meridian and a moving orbital witness.
- **Rally (reserve):** chalk case, rubber tread, clay stripe and a broad yellow key.

Existing finishes keep their identifiers and distinctive construction. Groove gains the offset arm; Field gains tape; Aperture gains opening leaves; Foundry gains a linked gear; Signal/Correspondent gain short physical state changes. Glacier exposes a frosted drive, Cartridge a small cassette window, Switchboard a fixed finger stop. Vector separates its fixed glass/reticle from the directional tail. The quiet instruments receive material and edge refinement.
