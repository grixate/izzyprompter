## Revision 2 response

Removed all rejected tape, flag, stop and camera-lever overlays. Kept each stable skin ID. Removed Groove's parking support and Moss's extra recess.

Nothing Phone (3a)'s official design gallery was visually inspected: https://intl.nothing.tech/products/phone-3a . The useful principles are layered structural shields, a small number of defined material planes, captive screws, a thick clear perimeter and restrained asymmetry. This is an independent IZZY construction study, with no Nothing branding or partnership claim.

Clear and Spool now have fixed concentric stators and structural plates beneath transparent three-arm rotating flanges. The ivory round Spool hub replaces the square acid key. There is no fictional tape path. Foundry now has an actual 72-tooth rim and 12-tooth pinion sharing the same pitch. Rally gains three perforated steering-wheel spokes and a round centre hub.
